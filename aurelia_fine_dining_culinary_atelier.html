Our Story & Philosophy

A Sanctuary of Craft, Fire & Warmth

Founded in 2008 by Executive Chef Julian Vance and Sommelier Elena Rostova, Aurelia began as an intimate twelve-seat supper room celebrating seasonal harvests. Today, our kitchen unites classical European technique with bold wood-fired flavors and modern culinary precision.

We partner directly with organic coastal fisheries, heritage orchards, and artisanal bakers every morning. Whether you join us for a multi-course tasting menu, a hand-stretched sourdough pizza at the hearth, or a velvet dessert with espresso, every detail is designed for unhurried connection.
